Autonomy loop cost estimator

Size the issue-workhorse loop before you arm it. This turns the dials from COSTS.md into a number: how many sessions a day your cadence fires, and a monthly cost range for a flat subscription vs a metered API key. Defaults match the kit's config.example.yaml. The prices under the fold are editable — replace them with your own plans and observed per-session token use.

Worker tokens billed per active minute scale with work effort (low…max): 3k–9k / 5k–14k / 8k–22k / 12k–30k / 18k–42k. Reviewer: 15k–60k per pass, scaled by review effort. These mirror estimate_cost.py — keep the two in sync.

These are rough, order-of-magnitude assumptions, not a quote. Subscription runs cost nothing extra until you hit the plan's limits; metered runs bill per token, and real token use swings widely with prompt caching and how much each session actually does. Plans and prices move — calibrate with your own numbers before you raise the cadence.
