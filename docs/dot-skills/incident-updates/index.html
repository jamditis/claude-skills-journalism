Incident updates

One incident, one source of truth, clear staff guidance.

Portable workflow preview. Check available tools and approval requirements in your own environment before acting.

The workflow

  1. Establish an authorized primary source and the services affected.
  2. Separate confirmed availability from limitations, unknowns and role-test coverage.
  3. Keep the current status in one approved thread. Tell staff what they can do now and what to avoid.
  4. Update meaningful changes with their actual evidence time. Avoid repetitive unchanged recaps.
  5. Confirm recovery with runtime evidence before declaring an all-clear.

What this does not authorize

A communication skill does not authorize fixes, deployments, access changes, spending or new commitments. Follow the incident owner’s decision and existing communication scope.

Watch the wording

Planned maintenance is not a current outage. Missing exhaustive acceptance tests alone do not turn a working service unavailable. Never guarantee protection from unrelated problems.
