# surface-completeness skill

Check that a frontend change reaches every surface it affects: shared components and their
consumers, the relevant states and viewports, design-system compliance, and the docs that
describe the UI. Choose relevant quality signals from the linked reference, distinguish field from lab data, and record not-tested checks honestly.
